What was removed, and why

  • −The 01 / 02 / 03 markers. Three research areas are not a sequence, so the numbering asserted an order that does not exist.
  • −The profile card. Current, At, and Based restated the introduction sentence for sentence.
  • −The eyebrow above the name. It named a fourth research area, Explanation, that appears nowhere else on the site.
  • −The blog nav entry, the writing section, and the post counter. No route now leads to an empty archive.
  • −Separate Publications and Hobbies pages. One document now, with an anchor in the navigation for each of its six sections. This reverses M2’s move to page-to-page navigation; four of the twelve surveyed sites navigate the same way, and one file is one place for a fact to drift.
  • −The 96px page title. Twelve of twelve surveyed researcher sites have no display type at all.
